How it works

Three steps, no copy-pasting track names.

1

Open a playlist

Go to any open.spotify.com/playlist/… page in Chrome.

2

Click Convert to YouTube

Youbefy scrolls the whole tracklist and searches YouTube for every song, with live progress.

3

Press play

Your playlist opens on YouTube, in order, ready to loop or shuffle.

Two ways to convert

Pick one in the extension's options page — you can switch any time.

Default

Free mode

Zero setup. Opens a temporary YouTube playlist.

  • No Google account, no API keys
  • Plays in order with loop and shuffle
  • Beta: about 13 tracks per run for now
  • Temporary — not saved to your channel
  • 50 videos per tab (longer playlists open in parts)
Advanced

YouTube account mode

Saves a real playlist to your YouTube channel.

  • Named playlist: private, unlisted or public
  • No length limit beyond your daily quota
  • Uses Google's free API quota — no billing account
  • Needs your own Google OAuth Client ID (setup guide included)

FAQ

Do I need a Spotify Premium or Google account?

No. You only need to be able to open the playlist on open.spotify.com. Free mode needs no Google account at all.

Why is my free-mode playlist split into several tabs?

YouTube keeps at most 50 videos in a temporary playlist, so longer playlists open in parts of 50.

Why did free mode stop after about 13 tracks?

Youbefy is in beta. After a burst of searches YouTube asks for a bot check, and Youbefy stops instead of pushing through it. It opens what it found so far and remembers those tracks, so running it again later picks up where it left off. YouTube account mode converts full playlists.

Will it always find the right video?

It picks YouTube's top result for "title artist". Most tracks match; the summary tells you how many could not be found.

Does Youbefy collect my data?

No. There is no Youbefy server — everything runs in your browser, and settings stay in Chrome's storage.
